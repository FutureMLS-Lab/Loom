Reviewer response control plane

One conference policy.
Many verified rebuttals.

Create one Studio per conference cycle. Loom reads the official Call for Papers and linked author guidance, separates hard policy from response strategy, waits for your approval, then applies that policy to every Paper Rebuttal in the Studio.

Paste the OpenReview link. That's the whole job.

Venue and year are read off the submission itself; the Conference Studio is found or created, official policy discovery runs, and the paper package (PDF + every official review) is fetched. You approve the policy once — the paper joins and the rebuttal agent starts by itself.
